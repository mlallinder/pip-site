Pip

Pip is a personal desk companion that runs on a Raspberry Pi. It listens for a wake word, answers questions, reminds you of things, takes notes and reads out your day.

Google Calendar

Pip can read the owner's Google Calendar so it can answer "what does my day look like?" and give reminders before meetings. It asks for read-only access to calendar events and the list of calendars. It cannot change or delete anything, and it does not read email.

Pip is a private, single-user project. It is not offered to the public, and Google sign-in is used only by the person who runs it, on their own device.
