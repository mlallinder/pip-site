Pip

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Pip is a personal desk companion that runs on a device its owner controls. This policy explains what Pip does with data. In short: your data stays on your own device and your own accounts, and nothing is sold or shared.

What Pip accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only). If you choose to sign in with Google, Pip reads your calendar events and the list of your calendars, so it can tell you about your day and remind you of meetings. Pip never creates, changes or deletes events. Pip does not read your email.
  • Dropbox (files you choose). If you set up notes, Pip reads and writes only the daily note files in the vault folder you name, so spoken notes appear in your notes app.
  • Voice. Audio is used to hear the wake word and what you say. The wake word is detected on the device, and audio is not sent anywhere until Pip has been woken. Spoken requests are turned into text and, when a question needs it, the text is sent to an AI service to write a reply.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens, saved settings, reminders and queued notes are stored on the device Pip runs on, in files only the Pip service can read. Pip has no server of its own and does not collect your data centrally. Calendar details are used to answer you and are not copied to any Pip-run service.

Who data is shared with

Pip does not sell your data, show advertising or share data with third parties for their own purposes. Data goes only to the services you connect, to do what you asked: Google (to read your calendar), Dropbox (to save your notes) and the AI service that writes answers (the text of your request, and any calendar or note details needed to answer it).

Pip's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Remove Pip's access to Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to Dropbox in your Dropbox account's connected apps settings.
  • Delete the saved sign-in files on the device, or run the sign-out command, to stop Pip using them.
  • Delete notes and reminders directly from your vault or the device.

Children

Pip is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/mlallinder/pip-site/issues.
